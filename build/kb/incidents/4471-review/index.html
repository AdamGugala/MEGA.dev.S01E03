Problem: shared parser assumes month first

The shared parser interprets dates month first even for tenants whose locale writes the day first. This swaps day and month for ambiguous dates. The parser row above gives this problem’s report count and affected-tenant count.

Use the tenant configuration, not the browser’s locale. Day-first tenants belong to this group except for submissions through the month-first-hint form described below. A fix to that form’s hint alone would not repair the shared parser.

Problem: booking-v3’s hint disagrees with its form reader

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY. People enter the date in that order, but the form reads it in the tenant’s day-first order. The hint row gives this separate problem’s report count. In this snapshot it affects form submissions for alder.physio, copper.studio, and pale.harbour.

The distinction is the route through the product, not just the tenant: an app or email report from those same tenants can belong to parser instead. Changing the shared parser alone would leave the form-hint mismatch unresolved.

The mechanisms and classification are defined in src/lib/kb/causes.ts, using the locale and form from src/lib/data/tenants/ via src/lib/kb/locales.ts. The export contains no product implementation proving a deployed fix.

Triage: compare causes, not subject lines

The accepted decision 0001 and the triage prose use normalized subject matching. The supplied explorer actually compares causes. These are different rules:

  • Identical subjects can describe different causes: keep those reports apart.
  • Different subjects can describe the same cause: they can share a cause ticket.
  • An unknown tenant is not evidence of a shared cause. Investigate before merging; matching subjects or two unclassified reports do not establish a duplicate.
  • Look up the tenant, its locale and booking form, and the report’s channel before assigning a cause. Intake tags are guesses, not verified classifications.
